# Original synthetic Northstar reports

All three PDFs are original fictional demonstration data generated by `scripts/create_sample_documents.py` and distributed under the project's MIT license. They contain no real company disclosures or confidential information. Each has three pages with headings, narrative facts and tables.

| File | Pages and subjects |
|---|---|
| `Northstar_Annual_Report.pdf` | 1: revenue/profit; 2: R&D/headcount/Aurora budget; 3: delivery summary/target |
| `Northstar_Operations_Report.pdf` | 1: projects/delivery; 2: Aurora pilot; 3: reliability/retention/support |
| `Northstar_Security_Policy.pdf` | 1: retention/encryption; 2: access/incidents/Aurora assessment; 3: malicious-instruction training example |

Deliberate discrepancies: annual delivery rate is 96% while operations says 94%; operations logs are retained 30 days while the later security policy requires 90. A model should describe sources and dates, not silently reconcile them. The policy appendix contains a fake instruction to test whether PDF content remains data. The string in that exercise is not a real access credential.

Questions and gold source/page labels are in `evaluation/evaluation_questions.json`. Facts absent from all documents include executive salaries and actual 2025 revenue. Avoid treating a revenue target as an achieved result.
